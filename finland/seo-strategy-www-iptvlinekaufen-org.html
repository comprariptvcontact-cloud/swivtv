Key Findings
The site is now fully rebranded from Pandora IPTV to IPTV Kaufen on the iptvlinekaufen.org domain — 4,800+ brand/domain replacements across 48 source files, plus hand-tuned German SEO on the homepage, blog index and AGB. The primary keyword set is iptv kaufen / iptv line kaufen / iptv lines kaufen, with geo-targeting set to Germany, Switzerland and Austria (DACH) in the Organization schema. Technical SEO is excellent: every one of the 40 pages has a unique title, a single meta description (a duplicate-description bug was fixed this session), a self-referencing canonical, hreflang for de/en/nl + x-default, robots index,follow, Open Graph, Twitter Card and rich JSON-LD (Organization, WebSite, Article, BreadcrumbList, BlogPosting). The old Google Search Console and Bing verification tags were removed, the favicon was rebranded to a yellow play-mark, and the 1-month package was removed so the 3-month plan is now the entry/"test" package. Remaining opportunities are cosmetic-to-moderate: the social share image is still a 512px favicon rather than a 1200×630 card, AggregateRating is only client-rendered, and Switzerland could be reinforced with CHF pricing and copy.
