Key Findings
Pandora IPTV is a technically well-built site with strong SEO fundamentals. All 10 page types have proper <title>, meta description, canonical URL, OG tags, Twitter Card tags, and structured data (JSON-LD). The keyword “pandora iptv” appears 3,551 times across 42 source files, giving excellent keyword saturation. Hreflang tags correctly target de, en, nl plus x-default. The sitemap index splits posts by language for granular Search Console reporting. The prerenderer generates full static HTML per blog post with Article schema, breadcrumbs, related posts, and prev/next navigation. Three areas need attention: (1) the OG image across all pages is a 512px favicon instead of a branded 1200×630 social image, (2) the 300-review testimonials section lacks AggregateRating schema, and (3) social media profile links point to generic platform URLs rather than actual brand accounts.

The highest-impact actions to take right now

Site-Wide Issues
Cross-cutting concerns that affect multiple pages
OG Social Image

All pages use favicon-512.png as og:image

Every page’s og:image points to /favicon-512.png (512×512). Facebook and Twitter recommend 1200×630. The blog posts use their Unsplash hero images (good), but the homepage, blog grid, and terms pages fall back to the favicon.

Create a branded 1200×630 social preview image and set it as the default og:image. Blog posts can keep their Unsplash images.
Critical
Social Media Links

Footer social links point to generic platform URLs

Facebook, Twitter, YouTube, and Instagram links in the footer point to the root platform domains (facebook.com, twitter.com, etc.) rather than actual Pandora IPTV brand pages.

Create brand social accounts and update the URLs. Also add sameAs URLs to the Organization JSON-LD in prerender.ts for Knowledge Panel eligibility.
High
Structured Data — Reviews

Missing AggregateRating schema for 300 reviews

The Testimonials component displays 300 reviews with a 4.9/5 average rating. This data is not expressed as AggregateRating JSON-LD, which means Google cannot show star ratings in search results.

Add AggregateRating schema: { "@type": "AggregateRating", "ratingValue": "4.9", "reviewCount": "300", "bestRating": "5" } within the Organization or Product schema.
High
