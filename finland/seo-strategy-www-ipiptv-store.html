Key Findings
The rebrand is technically watertight — 4,756 brand/domain replacements were applied and a full production build passes with every canonical, OG, hreflang and JSON-LD tag pointing at www.pandora-iptv.pro and every title/site_name reading PANDORA IPTV. The stray reversed form "IPTV Dark" and the pre-existing broken URL www.DARK IPTV.store were both fixed. Three things now hold the site back: (1) only 30 of ~380 written blog posts are wired into ALL_POSTS — the other ~350 (all the deBlogPosts*/enBlogPosts* files) are imported nowhere and ship to no one; (2) the Google & Bing verification tags were removed during rebrand, so the new domain must be re-verified and its sitemap resubmitted before anything indexes; (3) social links still point to the old instagram.com/pandora-iptv handle. Performance is the main technical watch-item: the client bundle is a single 665 kB (208 kB gzip) chunk.

Performance & Conversion

Single 665 kB JS chunk

Vite warns the main chunk exceeds 500 kB (208 kB gzip). On mobile this can hurt LCP/INP — Core Web Vitals are a ranking factor.

Code-split with dynamic import() (e.g. lazy-load the blog, checkout modal, motion library) or configure manualChunks.
Medium

Conversion tracking not configured

emailConfig.ts holds placeholder IDs and analytics.ts has an empty Ads conversion label — WhatsApp/registration conversions aren't measured. Not a ranking issue, but it blinds ROI.

Fill EmailJS IDs and the Ads send_to once the new Ads account exists.
