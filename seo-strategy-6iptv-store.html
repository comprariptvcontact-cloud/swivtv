Key Findings
The site's German blog (324 posts, prerendered via scripts/prerender.ts) is technically excellent: every sampled post carries Article + BreadcrumbList JSON-LD, full Open Graph/Twitter Card tags, a correct canonical, and a single alt-tagged hero image. The homepage is the glaring exception — its <body> is a bare <div id="root"></div> with no H1, no text, and zero links in the static HTML, and its title/meta target the brand name "6PTV" rather than your #1 priority keyword "6iptv" (Transactional/Navigational, 1,000 searches/mo). Cross-referencing your keyword list against the sitemap surfaced a bigger structural problem: the "iptv line" theme alone has 10 separate blog posts (iptv-line, iptv-line-2026, iptv-lines-ueberblick, iptv-testline, iptv-test-line, iptv-testline-ratgeber, iptv-line-test, iptv-line-test-anleitung, iptv-line-kaufen-2026, iptv-lines-kaufen-ratgeber), and the "german/germany iptv" theme has 5. Finally, the exact-match variants you flagged as priority — "iptv6" and "6 iptv" — have zero dedicated content anywhere on the site.

Blog post titles systemically exceed 60 characters
